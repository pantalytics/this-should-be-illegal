## What this means

- **Who wrote it.** The templates and skills were drafted by a non-lawyer, largely with the
  help of AI, based on the text of the law. Cited articles were checked against the official
  text, but checking an article is not the same as legal judgement.
- **Status.** Every template is `status: draft` until a lawyer named in `reviewed_by` has
  reviewed it. At the time of writing, none have been reviewed.
- **AI output.** The skills run on AI. AI output can be wrong, also when it quotes a source.
  Check every cited article and ruling yourself.
- **Your situation.** A template is a starting point for a common situation. Yours is
  different: statuten, tax position, other agreements and the facts all matter.
- **No relationship.** Using this repository does not create a lawyer-client or any other
  advisory relationship with Pantalytics, the maintainer or any contributor.
